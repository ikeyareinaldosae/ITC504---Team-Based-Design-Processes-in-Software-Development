# Revenue Calculations

## 1. Introduction

The revenue calculation module provides financial and operational performance indicators for the Movie Performance Management System.

The calculations use performance records retrieved from the shared PostgreSQL database and support both existing records and manually entered information.

All monetary values are represented in Australian dollars (AUD).

## 2. Implementation Structure

Revenue calculations are implemented through the following backend files:

| File | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| utilities/calculations.ts | Reusable calculation functions |
| utilities/dateUtils.ts | Reporting date calculations and validation |
| processors/revenue.processor.ts | Revenue aggregation and comparison |
| processors/weeklyData.processor.ts | Weekly performance aggregation |
| processors/chartData.processor.ts | Revenue chart data preparation |
| services/revenue.service.ts | Database-backed revenue analysis |
| services/comparison.service.ts | Individual movie comparisons |
| services/trend.service.ts | Historical revenue analysis |
| services/analytics.service.ts | Consolidated analytics results |

Frontend calculation, formatting and chart preparation utilities support the presentation of these results through the Analytics Dashboard and Performance Analysis pages.

## 3. Total Weekly Revenue

Total weekly revenue represents the combined revenue generated by all movies with performance records for the selected reporting week.

Formula:

Total Weekly Revenue = Sum of Individual Movie Revenue

Example for the reporting week commencing 12 October 2026:

| Movie | Weekly Revenue (AUD) |
|---|---:|
| Coral Kingdom | 66,800.00 |
| Midnight Harbour | 28,750.00 |
| Outback Run | 27,050.00 |
| Paper Lanterns | 18,750.00 |
| Southern Cross | 9,900.00 |
| Neon Static | 5,100.00 |
| The Last Ferry | 3,200.00 |
| Total | 159,550.00 |

Movies without performance records for the selected week do not contribute to the weekly revenue total.

## 4. Previous-Week Revenue

Previous-week revenue represents the combined revenue recorded exactly seven days before the selected reporting week.

For the reporting week commencing 12 October 2026:

- Current reporting week: 12 October 2026
- Previous reporting week: 5 October 2026
- Current weekly revenue: AUD 159,550.00
- Previous weekly revenue: AUD 167,850.00

If no performance records exist for the previous reporting week, the previous revenue value is represented as null.

The system does not substitute an earlier available reporting week for a missing previous week.

## 5. Revenue Difference

Revenue difference measures the monetary change between the current and previous reporting weeks.

Formula:

Revenue Difference = Current Revenue - Previous Revenue

Example:

159,550.00 - 167,850.00 = -8,300.00

The result indicates that total weekly cinema revenue decreased by AUD 8,300.00.

Positive values represent revenue growth, negative values represent revenue decline, and zero represents unchanged revenue.

## 6. Revenue Percentage Change

Percentage change measures the relative increase or decrease in revenue compared with the previous reporting week.

Formula:

Percentage Change = ((Current Revenue - Previous Revenue) / Previous Revenue) × 100

Example:

((159,550 - 167,850) / 167,850) × 100 = -4.9%

Percentage values are rounded to one decimal place.

When previous revenue is zero or unavailable, percentage change is represented as null to prevent division by zero and misleading comparisons.

## 7. Individual Movie Revenue Comparison

Individual movie revenue is compared against the same movie's performance during the previous reporting week.

Example:

| Metric | Coral Kingdom |
|---|---:|
| Current weekly revenue | AUD 66,800.00 |
| Previous weekly revenue | AUD 63,400.00 |
| Revenue difference | AUD 3,400.00 |
| Percentage change | 5.4% |

The comparison uses movie identifiers rather than movie titles to maintain consistent historical relationships.

## 8. Movie Revenue Share

Revenue share represents the percentage contribution of an individual movie to the total cinema revenue for the selected reporting week.

Formula:

Revenue Share = (Individual Movie Revenue / Total Weekly Revenue) × 100

Example:

(66,800 / 159,550) × 100 = 41.9%

Coral Kingdom contributed approximately 41.9% of the total cinema revenue for the selected reporting week.

Revenue shares are represented as null when total weekly revenue is zero.

## 9. Best-Performing Movie

The best-performing movie is identified by comparing recorded movie revenue for the selected reporting week.

The movie with the highest recorded revenue is identified as the best-performing movie.

For the reporting week commencing 12 October 2026, Coral Kingdom was the best-performing movie, generating AUD 66,800.00.

If no performance records exist, the best-performing movie is represented as null.

## 10. Historical Cinema Revenue

Historical cinema revenue is calculated by grouping performance records according to their reporting weeks.

The revenue recorded by all movies within each reporting week is aggregated to produce the cinema revenue trend.

| Reporting Week | Total Revenue (AUD) |
|---|---:|
| 7 September 2026 | 147,950.50 |
| 14 September 2026 | 163,070.00 |
| 21 September 2026 | 169,500.00 |
| 28 September 2026 | 164,950.00 |
| 5 October 2026 | 167,850.00 |
| 12 October 2026 | 159,550.00 |
| Total | 972,870.50 |

Historical revenue is presented in chronological order.

Missing reporting weeks are displayed as gaps in the frontend trend rather than fabricated zero-revenue records.

## 11. Individual Movie Performance Statistics

The Performance Analysis page calculates historical statistics for the selected movie.

### 11.1 Total Recorded Revenue

Total Recorded Revenue = Sum of All Recorded Weekly Revenue for the Selected Movie

### 11.2 Average Weekly Revenue

Average Weekly Revenue = Total Recorded Revenue / Number of Recorded Reporting Weeks

Only weeks containing actual performance records are included in the average.

### 11.3 Highest Weekly Revenue

Highest Weekly Revenue = Maximum Recorded Weekly Revenue

### 11.4 Latest Weekly Revenue

Latest Weekly Revenue represents the revenue associated with the most recent recorded reporting week for the selected movie.

### 11.5 Example

The following results are calculated using the recorded performance history of Paper Lanterns.

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Recorded reporting weeks | 5 |
| Total recorded revenue | AUD 103,050.00 |
| Average weekly revenue | AUD 20,610.00 |
| Highest weekly revenue | AUD 22,100.00 |
| Latest weekly revenue | AUD 18,750.00 |
| Previous weekly revenue | AUD 19,800.00 |
| Latest revenue difference | AUD -1,050.00 |
| Latest percentage change | -5.3% |

## 12. Operational Performance Indicators

The weekly analytics module also calculates operational indicators.

### 12.1 Total Tickets Sold

Total Tickets Sold = Sum of Recorded Weekly Ticket Sales

### 12.2 Total Screenings

Total Screenings = Sum of Recorded Weekly Screening Counts

If any required ticket sales or screening count is unavailable, the corresponding aggregate is represented as null rather than reporting an incomplete total.

### 12.3 Screening Status Summary

The screening status summary counts registered movies according to their current screening statuses.

The supported statuses are:

- Active
- Extended
- Ended

Verified operational results for the existing dataset include:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total registered movies | 8 |
| Active movies | 6 |
| Extended movies | 1 |
| Ended movies | 1 |
| Latest weekly tickets sold | 9,667 |
| Latest weekly screenings | 85 |

Screening status counts represent current movie statuses rather than historical screening statuses.

## 13. Currency Precision and Rounding

Revenue calculations use integer cents during aggregation to reduce floating-point rounding errors.

The following precision rules are applied:

| Value | Precision |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Two decimal places |
| Revenue difference | Two decimal places |
| Average revenue | Two decimal places |
| Percentage change | One decimal place |
| Revenue share | One decimal place |
| Ticket sales | Whole numbers |
| Screening counts | Whole numbers |

Frontend formatting utilities display currency using Australian dollar formatting.

Unavailable numerical values are displayed as N/A.

Rounded individual revenue shares may not always add up to exactly 100%.

## 14. Missing and Zero Revenue Handling

Missing revenue records and recorded zero revenue are treated differently.

| Condition | Calculation Behaviour |
|---|---|
| No current weekly records | Total revenue is zero and hasData is false |
| No previous weekly records | Revenue comparison is unavailable |
| Previous revenue is zero | Percentage change is unavailable |
| Current recorded revenue is zero and previous revenue is positive | Percentage change is -100% |
| Current and previous revenue are equal and previous revenue is positive | Percentage change is 0% |
| Total weekly revenue is zero | Individual revenue shares are unavailable |
| Missing historical reporting week | Display a gap in the revenue trend |
| Missing operational counts | Corresponding aggregate is unavailable |

These rules prevent misleading financial indicators and incorrect assumptions about missing performance information.

## 15. Manual Data Recalculation

Revenue calculations are based on current database records rather than fixed values.

New performance records contribute to subsequent analytics calculations. Corrected records replace previous values, and deleted records are excluded from subsequent calculations.

Live database integration testing verified the following changes:

| Operation | Weekly Revenue (AUD) |
|---|---:|
| Original database state | 159,550.00 |
| After inserting a performance record | 169,550.00 |
| After correcting the performance record | 174,550.00 |
| After test transaction rollback | 159,550.00 |

The tests also confirmed that revenue charts, movie comparisons and management review flags respond to relevant performance record changes.

## 16. Calculation Validation

Revenue calculation accuracy was verified through automated unit tests, processor tests, analytics service tests and live database integration tests.

The backend analytics regression testing completed 133 successful tests with no failures.

The calculation tests covered:

- Revenue aggregation.
- Positive and negative revenue differences.
- Percentage growth and decline.

Additional performance API testing confirmed successful creation, correction and deletion of performance records.

## 17. Limitations

The calculations measure recorded revenue rather than profit because operating expenses and production costs are outside the current system scope.

Average weekly revenue is based on recorded reporting weeks rather than every calendar week since a movie's release.

Historical screening status reconstruction is not supported.

Revenue calculations do not provide future revenue forecasts.

Frontend analytics updates require subsequent API requests or manual data refresh.

## 18. Conclusion

The revenue calculation module provides consistent financial and operational performance indicators for a single cinema hall.

The implementation supports weekly revenue analysis, historical trends, individual movie comparisons and management review decisions.

Automated testing has verified the calculation rules, handling of missing data and recalculation of analytics following changes to performance records.
